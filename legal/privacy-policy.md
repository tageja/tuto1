## 3. Student Data and FERPA Compliance

### 3.1 FERPA Compliance

Tuto Education Platform complies with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), which protects the privacy of student education records.

**What are Education Records?**
Education records include information directly related to a student and maintained by an educational institution or party acting for the institution, such as:
- Attendance records
- Grades and academic performance
- Discipline records
- Health records maintained by the school
- Special education records

**Our Role Under FERPA:**
- Tuto acts as a "school official" with "legitimate educational interests" when providing services to schools
- We access education records only as necessary to provide our Services
- We maintain the same privacy and security standards as the school

### 3.2 School Control of Student Data

**Schools Own Student Data:**
- Schools retain ownership and control of all student education records
- Schools determine what student information is entered into Tuto
- Schools control who has access to student information
- Schools can export or delete student data at any time

**School Administrator Rights:**
- Full access to all student records within their school
- Ability to add, modify, or remove student information
- Control over parent and teacher access permissions
- Right to terminate the school's use of Tuto at any time

### 3.3 Parent Rights Under FERPA

**Parents Have the Right To:**
- Inspect and review their child's education records
- Request corrections to inaccurate or misleading information
- Consent to disclosures of personally identifiable information
- File a complaint with the U.S. Department of Education

**To Exercise These Rights:**
Contact your child's school administrator or email us at support@tutoglobal.com

## 4. Children's Privacy and COPPA Compliance

### 4.1 COPPA Compliance

Tuto Education Platform complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for children under 13 years of age.

**How We Protect Children:**
- We only collect information from children with parental or school consent
- Children cannot independently create accounts
- We do not display third-party advertisements to children
- We do not collect more information than necessary for educational purposes

### 4.2 Parental Consent

**How Consent is Obtained:**
- **School Context:** Schools act in loco parentis (in place of parents) and provide consent for educational activities
- **Parent Registration:** Parents create accounts and provide consent when registering their children
- **School Admin Approval:** School administrators approve parent requests to link to students

**What We Collect from Children Under 13:**
- Name, grade level, and school
- Attendance records (recorded by teachers)
- Homework assignments and submissions
- Health information (with explicit parental consent)
- Photos in school activities (with school and parental consent)

### 4.3 Parental Rights Under COPPA

**Parents Have the Right To:**
- Review their child's personal information
- Request that we delete their child's personal information
- Refuse to allow further collection or use of their child's information
- Withdraw consent at any time

**To Exercise These Rights:**
- Contact your child's school administrator
- Email us at support@tutoglobal.com with subject "COPPA Request"
- We will respond within 30 days

### 4.4 Age Restrictions

- The App is designed for ages 4+ (App Store rating)
- Children under 13 cannot create accounts independently
- Parents must create accounts on behalf of children
- School administrators manage student accounts within their institution

## 5. School Data Ownership and Control

### 5.1 Data Ownership

**Schools Own Their Data:**
- All student education records belong to the school
- Schools can export their complete data at any time
- Schools can request deletion of all their data
- Tuto is merely a "data processor" - schools are the "data controllers"

### 5.2 School Data Isolation

**How We Protect School Privacy:**
- Each school's data is completely isolated from other schools
- Parents can only see their own children's data
- Teachers can only access students in their assigned classes
- School administrators control access within their institution
- No data sharing between schools without explicit permission

### 5.3 School Administrator Controls

**Administrators Can:**
- Add and remove users (parents, teachers, students)
- Control what information is visible to parents
- Manage parent approval for student linking
- Export complete school data
- Request deletion of school data
- Terminate the school's use of Tuto

### 5.4 Data Export and Portability

**Schools Can Export:**
- Student records (attendance, grades, health)
- Parent and teacher accounts
- Communications and announcements
- Photos and media (associated with the school)
- All data in machine-readable formats (CSV, JSON)

**To Request Export:**
Contact support@tutoglobal.com with subject "School Data Export"

## 8. Data Security

### 8.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

**Technical Safeguards:**
- **Encryption:** All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Secure Authentication:** Password hashing with bcrypt, OAuth 2.0 support
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access control (RBAC) to limit data access
- **Database Security:** Isolated school databases, row-level security policies
- **API Security:** Secure API endpoints with authentication tokens
- **Regular Updates:** Timely security patches and updates

**Organizational Safeguards:**
- Limited employee access to personal data (need-to-know basis)
- Background checks for employees with data access
- Regular security training for team members
- Incident response plan for data breaches

**Monitoring and Auditing:**
- Continuous monitoring for security threats
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Audit logs for all data access and modifications

### 8.2 Your Role in Security

**Please Help Us Protect Your Account:**
- Use a strong, unique password
- Do not share your password with others
- Log out of shared devices
- Enable two-factor authentication (when available)
- Report suspicious activity immediately

### 8.3 Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach:
- We will notify affected schools and users within 72 hours
- We will report to appropriate authorities as required by law
- We will provide information about what data was affected
- We will offer steps to protect affected users

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

### 9.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

**Active User Data:**
- Retained while your account is active
- Needed to provide Services to you and your school

**Student Education Records:**
- Retained as long as the school uses our Services
- Controlled by the school, not by individual users

**Inactive Accounts:**
- Accounts inactive for 365 days may be flagged for deletion
- We will notify you before deleting inactive accounts
- You can reactivate your account to prevent deletion

**Communications and Media:**
- Retained while your account is active
- May be retained longer if required for legal compliance

**Analytics Data:**
- Anonymized analytics retained for 90 days
- Aggregated (non-identifiable) data retained indefinitely for service improvement

### 9.2 Deletion Requests

**Individual Users:**
- You can request deletion of your personal account
- Email support@tutoglobal.com with subject "Account Deletion Request"
- We will process within 30 days

**Parents:**
- Can request deletion of their child's data
- Must coordinate with school administrator
- Email support@tutoglobal.com with subject "Student Data Deletion"

**Schools:**
- School administrators can request deletion of all school data
- We will export data before deletion (if requested)
- Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone

### 9.3 Grace Period

**30-Day Grace Period:**
- Deleted accounts enter a 30-day grace period
- You can cancel the deletion request during this time
- After 30 days, deletion is permanent
- Backups may retain data for up to 90 days (for disaster recovery)

### 9.4 Legal Retention

**We May Retain Data Longer If:**
- Required by law or regulation
- Needed for ongoing legal proceedings
- Necessary to prevent fraud or abuse
- Required to enforce our Terms of Service

## 11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

### 11.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you use our web dashboard. They help us provide and improve our Services.

### 11.2 How We Use Cookies

**Essential Cookies** (Cannot be disabled):
- **Authentication:** Keep you logged in to your account
  - Name: `sb-*-auth-token` (Supabase session)
  - Duration: Session-based or as long as you stay logged in
  - Purpose: Authenticate your identity

**Functional Cookies** (Can be disabled, but affects functionality):
- **Language Preference:** Remember your language choice (Vietnamese or English)
  - Name: `lang`
  - Duration: 365 days
  - Purpose: Display interface in your preferred language

### 11.3 Mobile App

**The Mobile App Does NOT Use Cookies:**
- No browser cookies in the native mobile app
- Uses secure local storage for authentication tokens
- Uses device preferences for settings (language, theme)

### 11.4 Analytics

**We Use Analytics to Understand Usage:**
- Firebase Analytics (optional, can be disabled)
- Anonymized usage patterns
- No personally identifiable information in analytics

### 11.5 Third-Party Cookies

**We Do NOT Use:**
- Advertising cookies
- Social media tracking cookies
- Third-party marketing cookies

### 11.6 Your Cookie Choices

**Web Dashboard:**
- Essential cookies cannot be disabled (required for login)
- You can clear cookies in your browser settings
- You can block third-party cookies in your browser

**Mobile App:**
- You can disable analytics in app settings (coming soon)
- You can reset your device identifier (in device settings)
