## 1. Why We Retain Data

We retain data for the following legitimate purposes:

**To Provide Services:**
- Maintain your account and preferences
- Facilitate school communication
- Track student progress and attendance
- Provide access to school information

**To Improve Services:**
- Understand how users interact with the app
- Identify and fix bugs
- Develop new features
- Improve user experience

**Legal and Regulatory Compliance:**
- Comply with FERPA (student education records)
- Comply with COPPA (children under 13)
- Meet financial and tax requirements
- Respond to legal requests

**Safety and Security:**
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Protect user safety
- Investigate security incidents
- Enforce our Terms of Service

## 2. Data Retention Periods

### 2.1 Active User Data

**While Your Account is Active:**
- **User Profile:** Name, email, role, preferences
- **Retention:** Retained while account is active
- **Purpose:** Provide Services

**Student Education Records:**
- **Data:** Attendance, grades, homework, health records
- **Retention:** Controlled by school (not individual users)
- **Purpose:** Educational services, FERPA compliance

**Communications:**
- **Data:** Messages, announcements, comments
- **Retention:** While account is active
- **Purpose:** School communication

**Photos and Media:**
- **Data:** User-uploaded photos and videos
- **Retention:** While account is active or until deleted by user/school
- **Purpose:** Document school activities

### 2.2 Inactive Accounts

**Accounts Inactive for 365+ Days:**
- We may flag your account for deletion
- We will email you 30 days before deletion
- You can log in to prevent deletion
- After deletion, 30-day grace period applies

### 2.3 Deleted Account Data

**After You Delete Your Account:**

**Personal User Data (30 days):**
- Your profile, settings, and preferences
- Deleted from active systems within 30 days
- May remain in backups for up to 90 days

**User-Generated Content (30 days):**
- Your posts, comments, and messages
- Anonymized or deleted within 30 days
- School-owned content may be retained by school

**Student Education Records:**
- Controlled by school, not deleted with parent/teacher accounts
- Parents must request deletion through school administrator
- Schools maintain records per their retention policies

### 2.4 Legal and Compliance Data

**Audit Logs (7 years):**
- System access and modification logs
- Required for regulatory compliance
- Anonymized after retention period

**Financial Records (7 years):**
- Transaction records (when payment features are active)
- Required for tax and financial reporting
- Currently not applicable (app is free)

**Security Logs (90 days):**
- Login attempts, security events
- Used for fraud prevention
- Automatically deleted after 90 days

### 2.5 Analytics Data

**Usage Analytics (90 days):**
- Anonymized usage statistics
- Feature usage patterns
- Performance metrics
- Crash reports and error logs

**Aggregated Data (Indefinitely):**
- Completely anonymized, non-identifiable data
- Used for service improvement
- Cannot be traced back to individuals

**You Can:**
- Object to certain data processing

## 4. How to Request Deletion

### 4.1 Individual User Deletion

**To Delete Your Personal Account:**

1. **Send Email Request:**
   - To: support@tutoglobal.com
   - Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
   - Include: Your name, email, school (if applicable)

2. **We Will Confirm:**
   - Within 48 hours via email
   - Provide deletion timeline
   - Explain what will be deleted

3. **30-Day Grace Period:**
   - Your account is deactivated
   - Data is not deleted yet
   - You can cancel the request

4. **Permanent Deletion:**
   - After 30 days, deletion is permanent
   - Data removed from active systems
   - Backups retained for up to 90 days

**Note:** Student education records are not deleted with parent/teacher accounts. See Section 4.2.

### 4.2 Student Data Deletion

**Parents Requesting Child's Data Deletion:**

1. **Coordinate with School:**
   - Schools own student education records (FERPA)
   - School administrator approval required

2. **Submit Request:**
   - Email: support@tutoglobal.com
   - Subject: "Student Data Deletion"
   - Include: Child's name, school, parent verification

3. **We Will:**
   - Verify with school administrator
   - Process deletion after school approval
   - Confirm completion via email

### 4.3 School Data Deletion

**School Administrators:**

1. **Request School Data Deletion:**
   - Email: support@tutoglobal.com
   - Subject: "School Data Deletion"
   - Include: School name, administrator verification

2. **Export Option:**
   - We offer data export before deletion
   - Complete school data in portable format

3. **Processing:**
   - 30-day grace period
   - Complete deletion of all school data
   - All associated users lose access

## 6. School Data Retention

### 6.1 School Ownership

**Schools Own Their Data:**
- All student education records
- School-created content
- Communications within the school
- School media and attachments

**Tuto's Role:**
- We are a "data processor"
- Schools are the "data controller"
- We hold data on behalf of schools

### 6.2 School Retention Policies

**Schools Determine:**
- How long to retain student records
- When student data is deleted
- What data is collected
- Who has access to data

**Our Responsibility:**
- Follow school's retention instructions
- Delete data when school requests
- Export data for school at any time

### 6.3 Student Transfers

**When Students Transfer Schools:**
- Data may be transferred to new school (with consent)
- Original school may retain records per policy
- Parents can request data deletion from old school

### 6.4 School Closure

**If a School Closes:**
- School has 60 days to export all data
- After 60 days, we delete all school data
- Parents notified in advance to save data

## 7. Student Data (FERPA)

### 7.1 FERPA Compliance

**FERPA Protects:**
- Student education records
- Personally identifiable information
- Academic performance data
- Behavioral records

**Our FERPA Obligations:**
- Maintain privacy of education records
- Only disclose with consent or as allowed by law
- Allow parents to review and correct records
- Delete records when requested by school

### 7.2 Retention Under FERPA

**While Student is Enrolled:**
- Records retained for educational purposes
- Accessible to authorized school personnel
- Parents can access their child's records

**After Student Leaves:**
- Schools determine retention period
- May retain transcripts permanently
- Other records may be deleted per school policy

### 7.3 Parental Rights Under FERPA

**Parents Can:**
- Inspect and review records
- Request amendments to inaccurate records
- Consent to disclosures
- File complaints with Department of Education

## 8. Children's Data (COPPA)

### 8.1 COPPA Compliance

**For Children Under 13:**
- Parental consent required for data collection
- Schools may provide consent (in loco parentis)
- Limited data collection (only what's necessary)
- No behavioral advertising
- Parental access and deletion rights

### 8.2 Data Collected from Children

**We Collect Only:**
- Name and grade (for identification)
- School association
- Attendance records (recorded by teachers)
- Homework and assignments
- Health information (with explicit parental consent)
- Photos in school activities (with consent)

**We Do NOT Collect:**
- Social security numbers
- Precise geolocation
- Photos/videos for non-educational purposes
- Personal contact information

### 8.3 Parental Control Under COPPA

**Parents Can:**
- Review all data collected from their child
- Refuse further collection
- Delete their child's data
- Withdraw consent at any time

**How to Exercise:**
- Contact school administrator
- Email support@tutoglobal.com with "COPPA Request"

## 9. Data Deletion Process

### 9.1 What Gets Deleted

**Immediately Deleted:**
- Optional photos/media you uploaded
- Non-essential user-generated content
- Preference settings

**Deleted Within 30 Days:**
- Personal profile information
- Account credentials
- Communications (messages, comments)
- Non-education records

**Deleted When Required by School:**
- Student education records
- School-owned content
- Official school communications

**Not Deleted:**
- Anonymized analytics data
- Audit logs (required by law for 7 years)
- Backup copies (for up to 90 days)

### 9.2 Deletion Methods

**Secure Deletion:**
- Data overwritten with random data
- Database records permanently removed
- Backup copies expire naturally

**Anonymization:**
- Personal identifiers replaced with `[DELETED]`
- Data no longer traceable to individual
- Preserves system integrity

### 9.3 Verification

**We Confirm Deletion:**
- Email confirmation when deletion complete
- Documentation of what was deleted
- Response to follow-up inquiries

## 10. Backup and Recovery Data

### 10.1 Backup Purpose

**We Maintain Backups For:**
- Disaster recovery
- System failures
- Data corruption protection
- Business continuity

**Backups Are:**
- Encrypted and secure
- Not directly accessible
- Automatically deleted after retention period

### 10.2 Backup Retention

**Standard Backups:**
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Retention:** 30 days
- **Auto-deletion:** After 30 days

**Disaster Recovery Backups:**
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Retention:** 90 days
- **Auto-deletion:** After 90 days

### 10.3 Deleted Data in Backups

**When You Delete Your Account:**
- Data removed from active systems immediately
- Backup copies remain until natural expiration (up to 90 days)
- Cannot be recovered or accessed during this time
- Automatically deleted when backup expires

## 11. Exceptions to Deletion

We may retain data longer than stated if required for:

### 11.1 Legal Obligations

**We May Retain Data If:**
- Required by law or regulation
- Subject to legal hold or preservation order
- Needed for ongoing legal proceedings
- Required by court order or subpoena

**Examples:**
- Tax records (7 years)
- Financial records (7 years)
- Audit logs (7 years)
- Legal dispute records

### 11.2 Fraud Prevention

**We May Retain Data To:**
- Investigate suspected fraud or abuse
- Prevent future violations
- Protect other users
- Cooperate with law enforcement

**Retention Period:**
- As long as necessary for investigation
- Up to 7 years for serious violations

### 11.3 Terms of Service Enforcement

**We May Retain Data To:**
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Investigate violations
- Ban repeat offenders
- Prevent abuse of Services

### 11.4 Anonymous Data

**We May Retain:**
- Completely anonymized, aggregated data
- Statistical data that cannot identify individuals
- Usage patterns for service improvement

**This Data:**
- Cannot be traced back to you
- Is not subject to deletion requests
- Helps improve Services for everyone
