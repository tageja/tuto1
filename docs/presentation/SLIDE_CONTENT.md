## SLIDE 2: About Tuto

### What is Tuto?

Tuto is a comprehensive **bilingual education platform** (Vietnamese & English) designed to:

- **Connect** schools, teachers, and parents seamlessly
- **Digitize** day-to-day school operations
- **Empower** parents with real-time visibility into their child's education
- **Reduce** administrative burden on teachers and staff

**Mission**: Make school management effortless while keeping parents deeply connected to their child's learning journey.

---

## SLIDE 3: The Problem We're Solving

### Challenges Schools Face Today

**For School Administrators:**
- 📋 Scattered systems for different tasks (attendance, fees, communication)
- 📝 Manual paperwork taking hours each day
- 📱 No centralized communication with parents
- 📊 Difficulty tracking school-wide metrics and trends

**For Teachers:**
- ⏰ Time wasted on administrative tasks instead of teaching
- 📧 Inefficient parent communication (Zalo groups, paper notes)
- 📚 No easy way to share homework and track completion

**For Parents:**
- ❓ Lack of visibility into child's daily activities
- 📞 Difficulty reaching teachers when needed
- 💰 Unclear payment schedules and history
- 📉 No centralized view of child's progress
