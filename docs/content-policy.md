# Tuto Community Guidelines & Content Policy

## 🌟 Our Mission
Tuto is a safe, supportive community where students, parents, and teachers can connect for quality education. We're committed to maintaining a positive environment that fosters learning and growth.

## 📋 Community Standards

### ✅ What We Encourage
- **Respectful Communication**: Treat all community members with kindness and respect
- **Educational Content**: Share helpful study tips, resources, and educational insights
- **Constructive Feedback**: Provide honest, helpful reviews and feedback
- **Supportive Environment**: Help fellow students and celebrate achievements
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Respect diverse backgrounds, languages, and learning styles

### ❌ What We Don't Allow

#### Harassment & Bullying
- Personal attacks, threats, or intimidation
- Cyberbullying or trolling behavior
- Discriminatory language based on race, gender, religion, or other protected characteristics
- Stalking or unwanted contact

#### Inappropriate Content
- Sexual, violent, or graphic content
- Hate speech or discriminatory language
- Spam, scams, or misleading information
- Content that promotes illegal activities
- Personal information sharing (addresses, phone numbers, etc.)

#### Academic Integrity
- Plagiarism or academic dishonesty
- Sharing exam answers or cheating materials
- False credentials or qualifications
- Misleading educational claims

#### Commercial Abuse
- Unauthorized advertising or promotion
- Spam or repetitive commercial content
- Pyramid schemes or get-rich-quick schemes
- Selling services outside the platform

## 🚨 Reporting & Moderation

### How to Report
If you encounter content or behavior that violates our guidelines:

1. **Report Posts**: Tap the flag icon on any post
2. **Report Users**: Use the report option in user profiles
3. **Contact Support**: Email us at support@tuto.app for serious issues

### What Happens Next
- Reports are reviewed by our moderation team within 24 hours
- Violating content is removed immediately
- Repeat offenders may face temporary or permanent restrictions
- We'll notify you of the outcome when possible

### False Reports
- Intentionally false reports waste our resources and harm the community
- Users who abuse the reporting system may face restrictions
- Please only report genuine violations

## 🛡️ User Safety

### Blocking Users
- You can block any user to prevent them from contacting you
- Blocked users cannot see your posts or send you messages
- You can unblock users at any time in your settings

### Privacy Protection
- Never share personal information (addresses, phone numbers, etc.)
- Use the platform's messaging system for communication
- Report any attempts to move conversations off-platform

### Age-Appropriate Content
- All content must be appropriate for our diverse age range
- Parents can monitor their children's activity
- Teachers must maintain professional boundaries

## 📚 Educational Standards

### Teacher Expectations
- Provide accurate, helpful educational content
- Maintain professional communication with students and parents
- Respect student privacy and confidentiality
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations

### Student & Parent Guidelines
- Be respectful to teachers and other community members
- Provide honest feedback and reviews
- Report any inappropriate behavior immediately
- Follow academic integrity standards

## 🔄 Appeals Process

If you believe your content was incorrectly removed or your account was unfairly restricted:

1. **Submit an Appeal**: Contact support@tuto.app with your case details
2. **Provide Context**: Explain why you believe the decision was incorrect
3. **Review Process**: Our team will review your appeal within 48 hours
4. **Final Decision**: We'll notify you of the outcome

## 📞 Contact Information

### General Support
- **Email**: support@tuto.app
- **Response Time**: Within 24 hours

### Safety Concerns
- **Email**: safety@tuto.app
- **Response Time**: Within 4 hours for urgent issues

### Legal Inquiries
- **Email**: legal@tuto.app
- **Response Time**: Within 48 hours

## 🔄 Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our community needs or legal requirements. We'll notify users of significant changes through:

- In-app notifications
- Email announcements
- Updated policy document
