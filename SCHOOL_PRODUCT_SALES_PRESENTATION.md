# Tuto School Management Platform
## Complete Product Overview & Sales Presentation

---

## Executive Summary

**Tuto** is a comprehensive school management platform designed to streamline communication, improve operational efficiency, and enhance parental engagement. Our solution provides schools with powerful administrative tools via a web dashboard and gives parents real-time access to their children's school activities through a beautiful mobile application.

**Key Value Propositions:**
- **For Schools**: Reduce administrative workload by 60% with automated tracking and digital forms
- **For Parents**: Stay connected with real-time updates on child's activities, health, and academic progress
- **For Teachers**: Simplify daily tasks with intuitive interfaces for attendance, homework, and student communication

---

## Platform Overview

### Two Complementary Applications

#### 1. **Web Dashboard** (for School Administrators & Teachers)
- Accessible from any desktop or laptop computer
- Comprehensive school management interface
- Real-time data analytics and reporting
- Multi-school support for educational groups

#### 2. **Mobile App** (for Parents)
- Available on iOS and Android
- Real-time notifications and updates

## Core Features

### 1. Student Attendance Management

**Admin Dashboard Capabilities:**
- **Digital Attendance Tracking**: Mark students present, absent, late, or excused with a single click
- **Real-time Analytics**: View attendance rates, patterns, and trends across classes
- **Automated Reports**: Generate attendance reports by date range (week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months)
- **Class-level Filtering**: Track attendance by specific classes or individual students
- **Visual Dashboards**: 
  - Present/Absent/Late/Excused summary cards with color-coded statistics
  - Weekly attendance calendar showing M-T-W-T-F status for each student
  - Attendance rate percentages automatically calculated

**Parent Mobile App:**
- View child's attendance history with visual calendar
- See attendance rate percentage
- Receive push notifications for absences
- View daily check-in/check-out times

**Business Impact**: Eliminates paper attendance registers, reduces data entry time by 80%, and provides parents with transparency.

---

### 2. Daily Activities Tracking

**Admin Dashboard Capabilities:**
- **Create Activity Records**: Document all daily activities including:
  - Meals (breakfast, lunch, snacks)
  - Learning activities (subjects covered, skills practiced)
  - Play time and physical activities
  - Rest/nap times
  - Special activities and events
- **Activity Types**: Pre-configured categories with custom icons
- **Photo/Video Attachments**: Add media to activities for visual documentation
- **Class Assignment**: Link activities to specific classes or individual students
- **Status Tracking**: Mark activities as Pending, In Progress, or Completed
- **Date Navigation**: Easy browsing of activities by date with calendar picker
- **Search & Filter**: Find activities by keyword, date range, class, or activity type
- **KPI Dashboard**: 
  - Total activities tracked
  - Completed vs. pending activities
  - Activity distribution by type

**Parent Mobile App:**
- View all activities their child participated in today
- See photos and videos uploaded by teachers
- Timeline view showing what happened throughout the day
- Activity details including time, duration, and teacher notes

**Business Impact**: Parents feel connected to their child's day, reducing anxiety and increasing satisfaction. Schools demonstrate transparency and quality care.

### 3. Announcements & Communication

**Admin Dashboard Capabilities:**
- **Create Announcements**: Compose rich-text announcements with:
  - Title and detailed content
  - Priority levels (Urgent, High, Normal, Low)
  - Target audience selection (all parents, specific classes, specific students)
  - Attachments (PDFs, images, documents)
  - Scheduled publishing
- **Announcement Management**:
  - Draft mode for preparing announcements
  - Publish immediately or schedule for later
  - Archive old announcements
  - Edit or delete announcements
- **Announcement Types**: General, Emergency, Event, Academic, Administrative
- **Status Tracking**: See read receipts and delivery confirmation
- **Search & Filter**: Find announcements by status (Published, Draft, Archived)

**Parent Mobile App:**
- Receive push notifications for new announcements
- Read announcements with full formatting and attachments
- Mark as read/unread
- Priority indicators for urgent messages
- Archive view for historical announcements

**Business Impact**: Instant communication with entire school community, eliminating printed notices and ensuring important information reaches parents immediately.

### 4. Homework Management

**Admin Dashboard Capabilities:**
- **Create Homework Assignments**: Full-featured assignment creation with:
  - Assignment title and detailed instructions
  - Subject/topic selection
  - Class assignment (one or multiple classes)
  - Due date and time
  - Attachments (worksheets, reference materials)
  - Point/grade weighting
- **Homework Tracking**:
  - Submission status (Not Submitted, Submitted, Graded)
  - Completion rate tracking (percentage of students completed)
  - Automatic overdue detection with visual alerts
  - Progress bars showing submission statistics
- **Advanced Filtering**:
  - Filter by status (All, Pending, Completed)
  - Filter by class, subject, or individual student
  - Search by assignment title
  - Date range selection (week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months)
- **Analytics Dashboard**:
  - Total assignments created
  - Pending vs. completed assignments
  - Overall completion rate percentage
  - Assignments due today
- **Grading Tools**:
  - Bulk grading interface
  - Individual student feedback
  - Grade export functionality

**Parent Mobile App:**
- View all current homework assignments for child
- See due dates with countdown timers
- Overdue assignments highlighted in red
- Mark homework as completed (pending teacher verification)
- View graded assignments with feedback
- Download attached materials
- Homework calendar view

**Business Impact**: Reduces parent complaints about "not knowing about homework," improves completion rates by 40%, and provides teachers with submission analytics.

### 5. Health Records & Medicine Management

**Admin Dashboard Capabilities:**
- **Student Health Profiles**:
  - Allergies documentation (food, environmental, medication)
  - Medical conditions tracking
  - Emergency contact information
  - Doctor's notes and recommendations
  - Health insurance details
- **Medicine Management**:
  - Create medicine reminders for students requiring regular medication
  - Medicine name, dosage, and frequency
  - Time-of-day scheduling (specific times like 9:00 AM, 2:00 PM)
  - Frequency options (once, daily, twice daily, as needed)
  - Active/paused/ended status tracking
- **Medicine Administration Logs**:
  - Log each medicine administration with timestamp
  - Record who administered (staff member name)
  - Notes field for observations or reactions
  - Photo documentation option
- **Health KPIs**:
  - Total students with health records
  - Number of students with allergies
  - Number of students on regular medication
  - Health records updated this month
- **Filtering & Search**:
  - Filter by class or individual student
  - Search by student name or medicine name
  - View students requiring medicine today

**Parent Mobile App:**
- View child's health profile and medical history
- See active medicine reminders
- Receive notifications when medicine is administered
- View medicine administration logs with timestamps
- Update emergency contact information
- Upload medical documents and prescriptions

**Business Impact**: Ensures critical health information is never missed, protects school from liability, and gives parents peace of mind about their child's medical needs.
